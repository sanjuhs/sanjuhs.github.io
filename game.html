In Legend of the Necro Knight, you play as a powerful warrior with the ability to revive fallen enemies and command them as your own. Explore an open world filled with monsters, gnomes, and mythical creatures.

Build your army strategically, choose formations, and watch as your forces auto-battle their way through hordes of enemies.
